# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The product has one local owner: a recruiter researching San Francisco Bay Area technology startups, validating the right hiring decision-maker, reviewing every record, and preparing deliberate one-to-one outreach. The owner works through a large queue but needs each company to feel like a researched account rather than a row in a purchased list.

## Product Purpose

Build and maintain an evidence-backed directory of up to 10,000 Bay Area startups with immediate hiring needs, the best current decision-maker at each company, verified contact routes, and a complete audit trail. The product combines permitted public-web research and licensed enrichment providers, ranks companies by hiring intent, makes conflicts visible, supports line-by-line human review, prepares tailored outreach drafts, and exports clean data without publishing the underlying company database.

Success means the owner can move quickly from discovery to a trustworthy reviewed record, always understand why a company and person were selected, safely resume work, and avoid duplicate research or duplicate outreach.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, decisive. The interface should feel like a focused research desk: compact enough for sustained daily work, candid about uncertainty, and confident without becoming salesy or theatrical.

## Anti-references

- Bloated enterprise CRMs with deep configuration trees and duplicate concepts.
- Sales-engagement dashboards that gamify activity volume instead of showing evidence quality.
- Dark neon “hacker” tools, generic AI gradients, glassmorphism, and novelty interfaces.
- Spreadsheet replicas that expose every field at once and make review feel like data entry.
- Multi-step wizards for actions that can be completed in one clear screen.

## Design Principles

1. **One obvious next action.** Every screen centers the next useful decision: discover, resolve, review, approve, or export.
2. **Evidence before confidence.** Show source, freshness, and conflicts beside important facts; never hide uncertainty behind a single opaque score.
3. **Progressive depth.** Lists stay compact, while one reusable workspace reveals the full company, people, verification, hiring, and outreach context.
4. **Automation proposes; the owner decides.** The system may rank, infer, and draft, but every record remains editable and reviewable with a complete history.
5. **Local by default.** Data, credentials, screenshots, and logs remain in a git-ignored local data directory unless the owner deliberately exports them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, labels, status announcements, and target sizes. Never encode confidence or status by color alone. Honor reduced-motion preferences. Dense research views must remain usable at 200% zoom and on a laptop-width viewport.
